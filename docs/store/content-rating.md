# Content rating (IARC questionnaire in Play Console)

Where: Play Console > Policy and programs > App content > Content rating. Choose the category **Utility,
Productivity, Communication or Other** (FORM is not a game, social network or store). The questionnaire changes over
time: read each question and answer for what FORM does today. The answers below are what the app supports.

Expected outcome: a low rating (PEGI 3 / ESRB Everyone / IARC 3+ is typical for this answer set). Do not argue
with the computed rating; if it comes out higher than expected, re-check the answers, not the rating.

| Topic | Answer | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Violence (cartoon, fantasy or realistic) | No | None. The app shows exercise artwork and food photos only |
| Blood or gore | No | None |
| Sexual content or nudity | No | None. Progress photos are private, on-device, never shared or uploaded |
| Profanity or crude humor | No | None. Leaderboard names are filtered |
| Controlled substances (drugs, alcohol, tobacco) | No | Not depicted or promoted. Food data may include alcoholic drinks as nutrition entries only; if the questionnaire asks specifically, still answer No for depiction or promotion |
| Gambling or simulated gambling | No | None. XP and ranks have no cash value and cannot be bought or sold |
| Real-money purchases or ads | No ads. No in-app purchases yet | Revisit when FORM Pro is sold and answer "Yes, in-app purchases" then |
| User-generated content shared with other users | Limited: leaderboard display name, only if the user opts in | Names are filtered; no chat, no photo sharing, no profile browsing. If asked "can users interact", answer: users can see each other's chosen display name and rank on a leaderboard and nothing else. Reporting or moderation: names are checked automatically; no free-text posts |
| Users can communicate with each other | No | No messaging between users |
| Shares the user's location with others | No | No location access at all |
| Digital purchases | No (yet) | See above |
| Unrestricted web access | No | No in-app browser; legal links open the system browser |
| Health, medical or fitness claims | Fitness and nutrition tracking only; not a medical device, no diagnosis | The listing and policy say it is not medical advice |
| Interactive elements (the IARC "interactive elements" line) | Users interact: Yes (leaderboard names). Shares info: Yes (opt-in display name). Digital purchases: No. Location: No | |

Target audience (separate form): **13 and over**. Do not select children's age groups; FORM is not designed for
or marketed to under 13s, and the app rejects an age under 13.

Keep the IARC certificate email for your records. Re-answer the questionnaire when you add Pro purchases,
user-to-user features or any content that changes an answer above.
