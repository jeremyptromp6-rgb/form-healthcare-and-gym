# Screenshots and feature graphic

Play requires at least 2 phone screenshots (up to 8). Use all 8 below. Specs (verify in Play Console): JPEG or
24-bit PNG, no alpha, 16:9 or 9:16, each side between 320 and 3,840 px, the long side at most twice the short side.
Capture at 1080x2400 (a modern Android phone, portrait) so no scaling is needed.

Rules for every capture:
- Real app, real device or emulator build with the latest release code, light OR dark but the same across all 8.
- Use a clean demo account with a believable week of data (a few workouts, meals logged, a streak, a rank above Rookie).
- No personal email, name or real face. Use a display name like "Alex". For the progress photo screen use a
  mannequin, a silhouette or the ghost-pose guide with the photo area blank; never a real person unless they consented.
- Hide the status bar notifications; set the clock to 9:41 and battery full.
- Food screenshots may show the licensed food photos (their credits are in the app under Photo credits). Do not add
  logos or brands that are not FORM's.
- No text claims beyond what the app does. No "#1", no medical claims.

Optional caption bar: put a short caption (below) in a band above or below the phone frame, in the app's teal and
charcoal. Keep each caption under about 6 words so it reads at thumbnail size.

## Feature graphic (1024 x 500 px, JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no alpha)

Concept:
- Background: charcoal (the app's dark surface) with a soft teal glow on the left third.
- Left: the word FORM in the app's heavy Inter weight with wide letter spacing, and the tagline
  "Rep counting. Form feedback. Meal scan." in a lighter weight below (three short phrases, nothing else).
- Right: two phone frames, slightly overlapping and tilted a few degrees: the camera rep-counting screen (shot 1) in
  front and the meal scan result (shot 4) behind.
- Keep the key content inside the central 924 x 400 area (Play crops the edges on some surfaces). No store badges,
  no download or rating text, no text smaller than about 28 px.
- Original artwork only: build it from FORM's own screens and the app icon. Do not copy another app's look,
  assets or logos.

## App icon

512 x 512 PNG, 32-bit, from `apps/mobile/assets/images/icon.png` (check the source is at least 512 px; the generator is
`apps/mobile/scripts/generate-icons.mjs`). Play applies its own rounded mask, so upload the square icon.
